Research

My research focuses on the distributed execution of agentic AI systems. I design runtimes that coordinate reasoning workloads across GPU clusters, optimizing for time to a correct and verifiable result. With a background in computer architecture and networking, I treat compute, memory, and interconnect as a single system problem. A central aim of the work is enabling organizations to run capable AI on their own infrastructure, under their own data governance.

Outside the lab

Outside the lab, I’m an avid skier and a former ski race coach, a committed home cook (sourdough, and Italian, Middle Eastern, and Japanese food), and a traveller whenever the research calendar allows. I founded Exam Tutors for Charity, and I’m a Silver Ambassador for the Ride to Conquer Cancer, training for the 200 km ride.
